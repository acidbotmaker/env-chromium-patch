Refresh clock adversarial harness

Runs the checks in PRD section 22 against this build and reports, for each, whether the model holds or breaks. Breaks are the result, not a defect.

1. Timestamp resolution

2. Cadence, idle

3. Load coupling (PRD 22.1)

Compares cadence statistics under light and heavy main-thread render load. Real hardware perturbs under load; a free-running clock does not.

4. Drop clustering (PRD 22.2)

5. rAF vs performance.now() (PRD 22.3)

Reads performance.now() at the top of the callback and subtracts the rAF timestamp argument. This design moves the rAF timestamp while now() stays real, so the delta may expose the injected jitter directly. The PRD expects this to be the strongest single detector.
